Welcome to Charlie's!

Grab a cozy cup of coffee or tea and spend time with our charming rescue cats at Charlie’s. We're more than a café — we're a home between homes for cats waiting for their forever families. Each month, meet three adoptable stars in our Three-Cat Spotlight from local shelter partners.

Adoptable Cats

Meet Luna

This graceful 3-year-old tuxedo cat is equal parts elegant and silly. Luna loves window sunbathing and will politely chirp when she wants attention. She’s gentle, affectionate, and great with kids — the perfect lap companion for a calm household.

Meet Mochi

Mochi is a playful orange tabby kitten with a curious spirit and boundless energy. At just 5 months old, he’s already mastered the art of stealing hearts (and the occasional muffin crumb). Mochi would thrive in an active home where playtime and cuddles are always on the menu.

Meet Chai

Chai, a soft grey senior cat with big green eyes, is all about quiet companionship. She’s calm, loving, and a big fan of naps near warm mugs. If you’re looking for a mellow best friend to share peaceful mornings with, Chai might just be your perfect match.
